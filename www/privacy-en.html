2. How Files and Input Are Handled

  • Tools that process on your device (tools marked "Processed on device" or "On-device AI" on screen — PDF editing, merging, and splitting, file conversion, background removal, image quality enhancement, removing people and objects, etc.): your files never leave your device (browser). They are not sent to the Service's servers either. On-device AI tools only download the AI model files once, the first time; your photos are not uploaded. The advanced models used on a PC (background removal, erasing, face restoration) are large, so they are downloaded from the public repositories jsDelivr and GitHub. As with visiting any website, your IP address and browser type are passed to those companies at that moment, but your photos and files are never sent. In "View & remove photo info", pressing "Open in map" passes the coordinates stored in the photo to a map site (OpenStreetMap) in the link address (nothing is passed unless you press it).
  • AI tools (tools marked "AI" on screen — meeting minutes, writing, research, document summarization and Q&A, text extraction, video summarization, AI chat, etc.): to generate results, the text and files you enter (recordings, images, documents, video links and captions) are sent to the AI services listed in Section 4. The Service's servers only relay the text and files you enter and do not store them. Original recordings and files remain only on your device. However, so that you can retrieve a result even after a refresh or a dropped connection, only the answer generated by the AI (the result text) is temporarily kept for about 1 hour and then automatically deleted. This answer can be retrieved only by the person who made the request (the same device or account).
  • If you do not log in, your work history, meeting minutes, and chats are stored only on your device (browser). They are deleted together with your browser data if you clear it.
  • If you log in, the text-based results and titles in the above records are stored in your account (up to 50MB per account). When you log in, any records already on that device are also uploaded to your account. If you turn on "Save on this device only" in Settings, nothing more is uploaded to the server, and records that were already uploaded are deleted from the server immediately.
Please be careful when using AI tools. During the current beta period, some AI processing uses Google's free usage tier. In this case, Google may use your input and the results to improve its own services, and Google reviewers may view them in a state separated from your Google account and key information. Do not enter company confidential information or sensitive personal information such as resident registration numbers, bank account numbers, or health information into AI tools.AI processing uses Google's paid usage tier. In this case, Google does not use your input for product improvement (training), and records it only for a limited period for abuse prevention and legal requirements. Even so, we recommend that you do not enter sensitive personal information such as resident registration numbers or bank account numbers. When recording a meeting, please inform the participants in advance.

2-1. Meeting Recordings — Files That Contain Other People's Voices

A file you record or upload in the meeting-minutes tool contains not only your own voice but also other participants' voices and remarks. Those are their personal information, so the Service handles them as follows.

Before you record (your responsibilities)

  • Before you start recording, tell the participants that you are recording and that AI will transcribe and summarize it, and get their consent. The recording screen of the meeting tool shows this reminder as well.
  • Secretly recording a conversation between other people that you are not part of is prohibited by Korea's Protection of Communications Secrets Act. Do not upload such files.
  • When recording a company meeting, check your company's security and recording rules first. You are responsible for the recording and for the participants' information in the minutes; the Service is a tool that helps you process them.

Where the recording is stored

  • The original recording (audio file) is stored only on your device (browser storage). It is not stored on the Service's servers, and even if you log in and turn on sync, the original audio is never uploaded. Only the text transcript and minutes are stored in your account.
  • For transcription the audio is sent to Groq (USA), and only when you press "Identify speakers" to Google (USA). The Service's servers relay the file and do not keep it. Each company's retention period is listed in Section 4.
  • So that you can continue if the browser closes while recording, the audio being recorded is temporarily saved on your device. It is deleted when transcription finishes or when you press "Discard".

How long it is kept, and how to delete it

  • The original recording stays on your device until you delete it. Clearing the site data in your browser deletes it as well.
  • Delete one meeting: meeting screen → More (⋯) → Delete. The recording, transcript, and minutes are deleted together, and if you are logged in, the synced text is deleted from your account too.
  • Delete everything: meeting settings → Delete all. All recordings and minutes on this device are deleted. Minutes synced to your account are deleted from the server when you delete them on the History screen, turn on Settings → "Save on this device only", or delete your account.
  • If a participant asks you to delete a recording, please delete it using the methods above. If a recorded person contacts the Service directly, the Service does not hold the original audio, so it will check and delete the synced minutes text in the account and, if needed, ask the user who recorded it to delete the file. Contact details are in Section 10.

5. Provision to Third Parties

The Service does not provide or sell your personal information to third parties, and does not use it for advertising. The only exception is where an investigative agency or similar authority requests it through lawful procedures in accordance with applicable laws.

6. Automatic Collection Devices (Cookies and Browser Storage)

  • The Service does not use cookies for advertising or tracking.
  • Your device ID, login token, work history, display settings, and similar data are stored in your browser's storage (local storage and IndexedDB). They are necessary for the Service to work, and are deleted if you clear site data in your browser settings (in which case records made without logging in will be lost).
  • If you use the Google login button, Google may use its own cookies to process the login, which is governed by Google's policies.
  • The Service does not collect behavioral information that infers your interests for the purpose of targeted advertising.

7. Your Rights and How to Exercise Them

You may at any time request access to, correction of, deletion of, or suspension of processing of your personal information, and withdraw your consent.

  • Deleting records: When you delete a record on the history screen, the synced record on the server is deleted as well.
  • Turning off sync: If you turn on Settings → "Save on this device only", the records on the server are deleted immediately.
  • Withdrawing membership: If you press Settings → "Delete account", your account information, synced history, point usage records, and vouchers are deleted immediately.
  • Records made without logging in: These exist only on your device, so you can delete them yourself in your browser. If you want usage records remaining on the server (keyed by device ID) to be deleted, please contact us with the device ID shown at the bottom of the Settings screen.
  • For any other request, please send it to the contact in Section 10. After verifying your identity, we will process it within 10 days and notify you of the result. You may also make requests through an agent.

8. Children's Personal Information

The Service is intended for users aged 18 or older (a condition of use of the external AI services), and does not knowingly collect personal information of children under 14. If we learn that information of a child under 14 has been processed, we will delete it immediately.

9. Security Measures

  • All communications are encrypted (HTTPS), and the information stored on the server is kept to a minimum. Only one-way encrypted values of IP addresses and login tokens are stored.
  • Each user's records can be read only when logged in to their own account.
  • The admin screen can be opened only by logging in with a Google account registered as an operator, and it shows only the account information (email, name) and usage statistics needed for operations and responding to inquiries. Users' work history and chat contents are not displayed on the admin screen.
  • Keys for using AI services are kept only on the server and are not sent to users' devices.
  • Information past its retention period is deleted automatically.
